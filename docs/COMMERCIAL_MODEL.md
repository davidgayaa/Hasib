# HISAB-AI — Commercial model and sales hypotheses
**Decision recorded:** 2026-10-18 | **State:** Design direction agreed; pricing and legal terms provisional.

## Market and positioning
- **Primary customers:** Moroccan accounting offices, fiduciaires, expert accountants and outsourced bookkeeping teams.
- **Product sold:** hosted accountant-first pre-accounting workspace with evidence-linked review, tax-aware controls, statement reconciliation and traceable exports.
- **Product stage:** blueprint only; no working software or customer evidence yet.
- **Primary promise:** measurable productive time saved and better visibility over exceptions. Never claim proven superiority without independent benchmark.

## Commercial structure
1. First-year package: initial acquisition/activation, configuration, onboarding and 12 months of managed platform service (contract must explicitly distinguish fee components).
2. From second year onward: **annual** managed service renewal for cloud access, maintenance, security updates, support, hosting/backup within limits and allocated AI/document credits.
3. AI extraction quotas, storage caps, included staff and dossiers are explicit. Customer-triggered upgrade or prepaid credit top-ups only; no surprise bills.
4. Annual renewal term and invoice collection state represented separately from the customer's accounting records.

**Not valid offers:** Quotas, taxes, exact feature gates and support response times need a cost model, interviews and approved terms before launch. First-year fee is not an automatic promise of perpetual software ownership.

## Unit economics that must be measured
Cloud hosting, PostgreSQL, private object storage, replicas and backups, AI inference per page and retry, queue operations, logs, emails/messaging, bandwidth, payment fees, customer onboarding, annual support hours, incident response, legal/privacy compliance, marketing and tax. Track realized gross margin per client and extreme usage.

## Customer retention / non-renewal
- At contract start disclose renewal date, included credits, contract duration and the distinction between hosted use and ownership of customer data.
- Expiry reminders (proposed 60/30/7 days, configurable; final wording subject to contract).
- Following overdue renewal: non-destructive service restriction, no deletions triggered solely by billing state.
- Proposed read-only/export transition (e.g. 30 days after a defined grace period) pending legal review, with options for a paid extension where permissible and secure migration support. Customer gets meaningful structured exports and source documents.
- Contract specifies lawful retention, deletion requests, accounting-record preservation, backups and any post-termination retention.

## Pilot validation
Interview 8–12 accountants on purchase behavior; pilot 3–5 firms. Measure pages/customer/month, average support hours, willingness-to-pay for first-year+annual model, effective renewal intentions, deployment preference, trust/privacy objections and export requirements.
